How it works

No forecasting knowledge needed. Here is the whole idea.

1Start with the history

Take a product's recent daily sales.

2Forecast what's next

The model predicts the coming days.

3Ask “what if?”

Hide the promotion days, or the recent days, and forecast again. The change shows what each part was worth.

4Get a plain answer

Typical pattern + promotion + trend, adding up exactly to the forecast.

wavexplain is a research tool. The toggles above switch between real model forecasts computed ahead of time, not numbers made up in your browser. They show how the model responds to hiding part of the input, not a claim about real-world cause.
